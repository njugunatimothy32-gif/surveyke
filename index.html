Quick help topics
How do referrals work?

Tap "Share on WhatsApp" on the dashboard and send your unique link to a friend. Once you confirm the message was sent, you earn KES 100. You need 5 confirmed referrals to unlock withdrawals.

Why is my account not activated?

You must send KES 100 via M-Pesa Send Money to 0734 872 045, then paste the confirmation message into the Activation section on your dashboard. Once verified, your KES 100 is automatically refunded to your wallet.

When will I receive my withdrawal?

Withdrawals are processed before midnight on the same day you submit the request. You'll receive an M-Pesa confirmation SMS once the money is sent.

I forgot my password — what do I do?

Password recovery is currently unavailable. If you can't remember your password, you'll need to create a new account using a different email address.
